What Cocoon does

Profile presets

ADHD, Autism, Anxiety, and Custom starting points you fully control.

Feed cleaner

Hides algorithmic feed surfaces on supported social platforms, with per-site overrides.

Sensory controls

Lightweight dark mode and reduced motion, applied only where you ask.

Grounding tool

A motion-free breathing timer and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding flow, one click away.

Scenario switches

Focus, low-stimulation, calm-reset, and social-guardrail modes that expire on a timer you pick.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no cloud. Settings live in your browser and nowhere else.

Supported sites

x.com / twitter.com, facebook.com, instagram.com, youtube.com, reddit.com, and tiktok.com.
